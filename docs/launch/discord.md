  1. Read `#rules` / the onboarding screen first.
  2. Look for a channel meant for sharing your work (names like `#showcase`, `#themes`, `#share-your-creations`, `#self-promo`, or a Forum channel with a "Theme" tag).
  3. **If no such channel exists, don't post the promo.** Support channels are for support. Answer questions about the theme when people ask, and that's it.
- Never DM members or staff to promote the theme, and don't ping roles.
